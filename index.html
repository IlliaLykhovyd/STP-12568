Game overview

Puzzles with a sense of adventure Royal
Adventure offers a familiar match-3 core enhanced by a fantasy setting. Each completed level contributes to restoring locations and unlocking new areas across the kingdom. You can actually see your progress - it's pretty satisfying watching the kingdom come back to life.

How it works

Here's how it works(It's pretty straightforward)

  • Match Gems

    Combine Three Or More Elements To Clear Them From The Board.

  • Create Power Moves

    Build Larger Matches To Trigger Magical Effects.

  • Complete Tasks

    Finish Level Objectives To Progress Further.

  • Unlock Areas

    Restore Parts Of The Kingdom Step By Step.

  • Advance The Journey

    Move Through New Locations And Challenges.

  • Collect Items To Improve Your Abilities.

    Gather Resources For Upgrades
